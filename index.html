What I do

I’m Angelo Michele Amoroso, a graphic and digital designer based in Caserta, Italy. I work across branding, UI/UX and service design, combining visual craft with research and systems thinking.

Experience

At Ratio Studio I developed visual identities and digital communication for different businesses. During my internship at Sidestream in Copenhagen, I contributed to a digital platform for food by-products through interface design, research and service analysis.

Background

My background combines studies in graphic and multimedia design at ABANA in Naples with a Master’s in Service Systems Design at Aalborg University in Copenhagen. I enjoy making complex ideas clear, useful and visually engaging.
