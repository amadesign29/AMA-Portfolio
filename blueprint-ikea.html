USER ACTIONS
Browse the catalogue, choose a cupboard and confirm the cart.
Enter delivery details and choose the IKEA Family bike option.
Log in with MitID, set driver preferences and wait for a match.
Approve payment, receive confirmation and track the delivery.
Share the security code with the driver and leave a review.
LINE OF INTERACTION
FRONTSTAGE
Product pages show options and colours. The cart brings the order together.
Compare delivery options and prices. Open the sustainable delivery page and accept the terms.
MitID verifies identity. The app offers filters and displays the driver search.
MobilePay requests approval. The app shows confirmation and delivery status; staff prepare the package.
The app displays the handover code, followed by a page for positive or negative feedback.
LINE OF VISIBILITY
BACKSTAGE
The IKEA system saves selected items and product details.
The IKEA system stores delivery information.
The broker returns identity data. The system saves preferences and notifies drivers until one accepts.
IKEA IT records shipment and bike details. The system tracks the bike to update delivery status.
The system updates the review count on the driver’s profile.
